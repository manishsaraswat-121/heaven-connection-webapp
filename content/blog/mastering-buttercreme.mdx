# Mastering Swiss Meringue Buttercream

Achieve a silky, luxurious buttercream finish for premium cakes.

Swiss meringue buttercream is prized for its smooth texture and stability. In this post we cover ingredients, temperatures, and finishing touches for a professional result.

## Ingredients

- Unsalted butter
- Egg whites
- Sugar
- Vanilla

## Technique

1. Gently heat egg whites and sugar until sugar dissolves.
2. Whisk into stiff, glossy peaks.
3. Add butter slowly while blending until silky.
