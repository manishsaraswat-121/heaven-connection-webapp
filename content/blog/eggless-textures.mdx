# Eggless Baking: Achieving Classic Textures

Premium eggless cakes are possible with the right balance of fats and aeration.

## Key Tips

- Use cultured dairy for richness
- Incorporate chemical leaveners carefully
- Use stabilisers when needed for mousse-like textures
